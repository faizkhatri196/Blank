1. The Operational Crisis in Solar Field Service
Market Grounding

Solar plants and distributed commercial/residential installations suffer from massive operational margin leakages. Service managers are forced to triage technical complaints manually over phone calls or WhatsApp with zero diagnostic telemetry. This produces severe economic and contractual friction:

30% – 42% Repeat Visits
Technicians arrive without specialized tools (MC4 wrenches, Earth Meggers) or critical spares (gPV DC fuses, SPDs), forcing second visits.
Tender SLA Penalties
Under Gujarat Government (GPCL) tenders, string and MC4 connector faults mandate 4-hour resolution. Manual triage breaches these SLAs.
Wasted Truck Rolls
Rolling a service vehicle for billing disputes or normal summer inverter thermal derating costs ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 with zero revenue upside.
